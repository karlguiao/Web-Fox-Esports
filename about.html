Our story

2023

The year Web Fox Esports was founded

Built to compete at the highest level

Web Fox Esports is a premier esports organization dedicated to competing at the highest level across a wide range of titles. Our players and teams are committed to showcasing their talent on the global stage, bringing fans unforgettable experiences and celebrating victories at every turn.

Founded in 2023, Web Fox Esports has grown quickly into a key player in the competitive gaming scene. Our mission isn't only to win championships but to inspire the next generation of gamers, create opportunities for collaboration and foster a positive gaming culture around the world.

League of Legends Valorant Counter-Strike Dota 2

Our vision and mission

Vision

To become a leading esports organization that excels in competitive play, community building and innovation.

Mission

To promote a strong sense of community, develop world-class talent and achieve excellence in every game we compete in.

We give players a platform to showcase their skills, connect with fans and build careers in esports.
